Subject: Pre-submission query: controlled-access clinical EEG dataset including minors, shared under an IRB consent waiver

Dear Editorial Office,

We are submitting a Data Descriptor, "Clinical scalp electroencephalography of 4,912 patients including multi-day ambulatory home recordings," describing a de-identified clinical EEG dataset already published under controlled access on the Brain Data Science Platform (DOI 10.60508/v99k-ek82).

Before you assess it, we want to flag one point against the Human Data policy. The cohort is an unselected clinical caseload and therefore includes children. The data were collected during routine clinical care; individual consent for data sharing was not obtained at the time. The Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center IRB (protocol 2022P000417) reviewed the project and granted a waiver of informed consent for this retrospective use, and approved release of the dataset in de-identified form with access restricted by a data use agreement that prohibits re-identification and redistribution.

The release is de-identified to the HIPAA Safe Harbor standard: all names, identifiers, dates of birth, case numbers, and technologist and equipment identifiers are removed; all dates are shifted by a random per-patient offset; names in free-text annotations are replaced by a placeholder; and only patient-level structured clinical fields are shared (age top-coded at 90, sex, diagnosis codes, medications, and reported EEG findings), with no free text or dates from the clinical records. Access requires registration, credentialing, and signature of the platform's data use agreement. These safeguards are described in the Methods, Data Records, and Usage Notes sections and in the Human Data Checklist.

We understand that the policy states consent waivers should not be used for data from minors. Given the IRB's determination, the de-identification standard applied, and the controlled-access route, we would be grateful for your guidance on whether the submission is acceptable as it stands, or whether you would require anything further.

Thank you for your consideration.
